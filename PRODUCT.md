# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Stack
User selected a shared phone interface: Flutter/Dart screens for Android and iPhone, Kotlin Android Bluetooth/foreground service and Swift CoreBluetooth/restoration bridges. DX340 host/source adapters stay Kotlin. Watch stays native SwiftUI. Native phone apps remain parity references.

## Users
The owner controls a DX340 kept in a bag from an iPhone or Android phone.

## Product Purpose
Bluetooth-only control and full browsing while installed Apple Music and Mango remain the playback engines on DX340. Watch controls follow stable phone operation.

## Operating Context
Only DX340 is currently USB-connected. Public GitHub Mac CI can build/run iOS simulators; no personal Mac or controller phone is currently available to this session. Physical acceptance is distinct from compilation and simulated tests.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Follow AGENTS.md and docs/implementation-plan.md. No everyday network dependency, root, firmware changes or private Apple downloads. Preserve Mango. Real source capabilities must be measured. User confirmed native system appearance.

## Evidence on Hand
docs/device-inventory.md and docs/capability-report.md track measured evidence. No radio interoperability or audio-quality acceptance yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large native playback targets, scalable text, screen-reader labels and explicit disabled/error states.
