# Phone asset origins

The iOS AppIcon and transparent LaunchImage PNGs are provisional, unmodified pixel assets from the official Flutter3.41.4 app template, flutter/flutter revision ff37bef603469fb030f2b72995ab929ccfc227f0, under packages/flutter_tools/templates/app/ios.tmpl/Runner/Assets.xcassets. Each PNG embeds that origin in text metadata. License: third_party/FLUTTER_LICENSE. The iOS launcher still uses the template Flutter mark and needs a product icon before release; no finished launcher-branding claim is made.

Android reuses the repository's existing ic_dx340.xml vector launcher mark. Abandoned generated Android Flutter icon PNGs were removed. Material icons are supplied by Flutter, and Cupertino glyphs by pinned cupertino_icons1.0.8; Flutter packages their font license information. No AI-generated image or new raster illustration was introduced.
