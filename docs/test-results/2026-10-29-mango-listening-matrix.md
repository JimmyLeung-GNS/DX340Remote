## Per-selection procedure and evidence

scripts/check-mango-listening-selection.ps1 takes one explicit Sequence, never a batch playback loop. It requires Apple paused, pauses only the preceding Mango clip, checks the unchanged reported volume/offline state, re-browses every storage/folder label with fresh opaque IDs and exact breadcrumbs, finds one exact playable filename, checks that public file's bounded container header and sends PLAY_ITEM once. It then observes matching Mango title/playing with Apple paused. A complete embedded title tag within the bounded prefix is used when available; otherwise the expected title remains a filename-derived hypothesis. A mismatch stops the attempt without replay. Successful source state and actual human listening are separate requirements. Filename/folder identity, app/package hashes, raw source reports, helper hashes and timestamps are retained per attempt under artifacts/mango-listening/.
